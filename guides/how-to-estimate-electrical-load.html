How to Estimate Electrical Load

Electrical load estimates help during early planning, but final conductor, breaker and service sizing must follow the applicable electrical code and project design.

Basic relationship

For a simple single-phase estimate, real power can be approximated as voltage × current × power factor. Apparent power is voltage × current in VA.

What to include

  • Lighting and receptacle loads
  • Motors and HVAC equipment
  • Kitchen and water-heating equipment
  • Continuous loads
  • Future or spare capacity where required

Why current alone is not enough

Motor loads, power factor, duty cycle and continuous-load rules can materially change the design. Nameplate data and the applicable code should take priority over a rough calculator estimate.

FAQ

Can I use this to choose a breaker?

No. It is a preliminary planning tool. Breaker and conductor selection require the applicable code, equipment characteristics and installation conditions.

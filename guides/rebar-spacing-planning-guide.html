Rebar Spacing Planning Guide

Rebar quantity estimates are useful for material planning, but the structural drawings and engineer's design control bar size, spacing, cover, laps and placement.

Estimate the bar count

For a simple uniform grid, divide the usable width by the specified center-to-center spacing and round up, then account for the starting bar. Edge cover reduces the usable dimension.

What the estimate does not decide

  • Bar diameter or grade
  • Structural spacing limits
  • Lap and development lengths
  • Concrete cover
  • Temperature or shrinkage reinforcement

Use the calculator

The Rebar Spacing Calculator gives a preliminary bar count from width, spacing and edge allowance.

FAQ

Can this replace structural drawings?

No. It is a takeoff aid only. Reinforcement design must follow the structural documents and applicable standards.

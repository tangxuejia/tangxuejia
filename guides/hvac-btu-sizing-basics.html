HVAC BTU Sizing Basics

BTU-per-square-foot rules can provide a rough starting point, but heating and cooling equipment should be sized from the building load rather than area alone.

What changes the load?

  • Climate and outdoor design temperature
  • Insulation and air leakage
  • Window area, glass type and orientation
  • Occupancy and internal heat gains
  • Duct losses and ventilation

Why oversizing can be a problem

An oversized air conditioner may cycle too quickly, reducing comfort and humidity control. A properly sized system can provide more consistent operation.

Use the calculator as a screening tool

Our HVAC BTU Calculator is intended for early estimates. Final residential sizing should use an approved load calculation such as Manual J or the applicable local procedure.

FAQ

Is 20 BTU per square foot a universal rule?

No. It is only a rough placeholder and should not be treated as a universal design value.

About

Less guessing at the store.

RenoMetric is a free calculator library for homeowners and DIYers who want a clearer way to estimate materials before buying.

What we publish

Each calculator combines a working estimate with a plain-English explanation of the formula, editable assumptions, and practical purchase rounding. The goal is not to create the largest possible number of pages. The goal is to make each indexed page useful on its own.

How the site is funded

RenoMetric is designed to be supported in the future by display advertising and, where relevant, clearly disclosed affiliate links. Monetization does not change calculator formulas or ranking recommendations.

Independence

RenoMetric is not a contractor, building authority, product manufacturer, retailer, or engineering service. Estimates are planning aids and should be checked against actual project conditions and product documentation.

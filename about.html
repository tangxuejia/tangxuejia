About RenoMetric

Less guessing at the store.

RenoMetric is a free construction and renovation planning library for homeowners, DIYers and early-stage project planners.

Editorial focus: help users move from a real-world project question to a transparent estimate and a safer next decision.

The problem we solve

People usually do not search for a formula. They search because they need to buy material, compare a project budget, check whether a quote makes sense, or avoid forgetting a practical detail. RenoMetric starts with that decision and works backward to the measurements needed.

What makes the tools different

Many calculators stop at a raw area or volume. RenoMetric aims to show the full planning path: the measured requirement, the purchase quantity after waste and package rounding, the assumptions that drive the result, and the checks that still belong with the supplier, installer or qualified professional.

Who the site is for

  • Homeowners preparing a renovation or repair budget.
  • DIYers estimating common materials before visiting a store.
  • Project planners comparing quantities, package sizes and visible assumptions.
  • Readers who want a plain-English explanation rather than an unexplained result.

How we keep the site independent

RenoMetric is not a contractor, building authority, product manufacturer, retailer or engineering service. It does not sell a required brand and does not hide a recommendation inside a formula. Product labels, supplier sheets, project drawings and local requirements remain the source of truth for final decisions.

How the site is funded

RenoMetric may be supported in the future by clearly disclosed advertising or relevant affiliate links. Monetization does not change formulas, editorial explanations or ranking recommendations.

Review and correction standard

We prioritize calculation errors, misleading unit labels, safety boundaries and pages that fail to answer the user’s main question. The methodology page explains the review gates used across the calculator and guide library.

Need to report an issue?

Use the contact page with the page address and the specific calculation or explanation that needs review.

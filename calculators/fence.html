How the fence calculator works

The fence run is divided by your maximum planning spacing, then rounded up to a whole number of sections. Posts are counted at section boundaries, with a separate input for extra corners or ends.

sections = ceil(run ÷ max spacing)

Example

A 120 ft run with an 8 ft maximum interval creates 15 sections before adding any extra corner or end posts.

Planning only: this calculator does not select code-required spacing, structural dimensions, footing depth, wind design, or gate-post requirements. Use the applicable design and local requirements.

Fence calculator FAQ

Why does spacing sometimes become slightly smaller?

The calculator treats your spacing as a maximum and divides the run into equal sections that do not exceed it.

Are corner posts included?

Use the extra-post input for project-specific corners, ends, or other additional posts.

Does this size the posts?

No. It estimates count and spacing only.

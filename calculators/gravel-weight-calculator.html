Why density matters

Gravel weight changes with rock type, moisture, grading, and compaction. Use the supplier's bulk density when available instead of treating the default as a guaranteed product value.

weight = volume × bulk density

Ordering note: suppliers often sell by the ton or truckload. Confirm the delivered product's actual density, minimum order, and moisture conditions.

How this planner works

It calculates slab volume, base aggregate volume and a two-direction rebar count. It then applies the visible allowance and multiplies the results by your optional prices.

Planning only: Rebar size, slab thickness, joint layout, subgrade preparation, concrete mix and local requirements must be confirmed by the project drawings, supplier or qualified professional.

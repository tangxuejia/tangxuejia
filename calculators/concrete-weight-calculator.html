Density assumptions

Normal-weight concrete is often planned around roughly 4,000–4,200 lb per cubic yard in US estimates or about 2,400 kg/m³ in Metric estimates. Actual density varies with aggregate, moisture, and mix design.

weight = volume × density

Safety note: use project-specific engineering and supplier data for structural, lifting, shipping, or load calculations.

Concrete Weight Calculator

Estimate the weight of concrete using project volume and standard density values.

How to calculate concrete weight

This calculator converts concrete volume into estimated weight by multiplying volume by material density.

Weight = Volume × Density

Worked example

A concrete slab volume can be converted into approximate weight by using the measured volume and the selected concrete density.

Why concrete weight matters

Knowing approximate concrete weight helps with transport planning, lifting limits, material handling, and project preparation.

Important note

Actual weight changes depending on mix design, moisture content, aggregate type, and local material specifications.

FAQ

How much does concrete weigh?

Concrete weight depends on volume and density. Use the project measurements for a better estimate.

Is concrete weight always the same?

No. Different mixes and conditions can change the final weight.

Why calculate concrete weight?

It helps estimate delivery requirements and handling needs before construction starts.

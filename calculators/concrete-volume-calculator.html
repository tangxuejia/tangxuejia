Rectangular volume formula

Use this page for slabs, patios, pads, footings, and other simple rectangular sections. Split irregular projects into measured rectangles and add the results.

volume = length × width × thickness
order volume = volume × (1 + waste)

Ordering note: ready-mix suppliers may require a minimum order and may recommend a different allowance for pumping, access, or site conditions.

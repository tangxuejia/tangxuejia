How duration is calculated

Production workdays equal total quantity divided by realistic daily output, rounded up. A separate buffer can cover setup, delivery, inspections, curing, weather or access delays.

workdays = ceil(quantity ÷ daily production)
planned days = ceil(workdays × (1 + buffer))

Planning only: production rates vary by crew, access, weather, material and site conditions. This is not a contract schedule.

How the kitchen remodel calculator works

The estimate combines kitchen area, average renovation cost ranges, and quality level adjustments. Actual prices vary by location, contractor rates, materials, and design choices.

Estimated cost = kitchen size × cost per square foot

Example

A small kitchen renovation with mid-range materials may cost less than a full custom kitchen with premium cabinets, appliances, and structural changes.

Major cost factors

Cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, plumbing, electrical work, and labor usually have the biggest impact on a kitchen remodel budget.

FAQ

Does this replace a contractor quote?

No. It is a planning estimate to help prepare a realistic budget.

Why can kitchen costs vary so much?

Materials, local labor rates, layout changes, and project complexity create large differences.

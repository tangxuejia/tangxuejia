Estimate asphalt volume and material requirements for driveways, roads, and paving projects.

How to calculate asphalt needed

Measure the length, width, and thickness of your paving area. Multiply these measurements to estimate the asphalt volume required.

Formula

Volume = length × width × thickness

Example

A driveway that is 20 feet long, 10 feet wide, and 3 inches thick requires calculating the total volume before ordering asphalt.

Tips

Always consider project conditions, compaction, and supplier recommendations before placing your final order.

FAQ

How much asphalt do I need?

Calculate the project volume first, then convert it into the quantity used by your supplier.

Should I add extra asphalt?

A small allowance may be needed depending on waste, compaction, and site conditions.

What measurements are required?

You need length, width, and thickness measurements.

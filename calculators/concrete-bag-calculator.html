Concrete Bag Calculator

Estimate the number of concrete bags required for your project based on volume, bag size and material coverage.

How to calculate concrete bags

First calculate the project volume, then divide the required concrete volume by the coverage provided by your selected bag size.

Number of bags = Required volume ÷ Coverage per bag

Common concrete bag sizes

Common options include 40 lb, 60 lb and 80 lb bags. Actual coverage depends on the product mix, yield and manufacturer specifications.

Planning example

A small patio project may require several bags after calculating slab volume. Always compare the result with the product label before purchasing.

Tips before buying concrete bags

  • Add a reasonable waste allowance for spills and uneven surfaces.
  • Check the exact coverage chart from your supplier.
  • Larger projects may be more economical with ready-mix concrete.

Concrete Bag Calculator FAQ

How many 80 lb bags of concrete do I need?

The answer depends on your project volume and the yield listed on the concrete bag.

Should I buy extra bags?

Yes, small projects often need extra material because of waste, uneven areas or measurement differences.

Can I use this for patios and slabs?

Yes. Measure length, width and thickness first, then convert the volume into bag quantities.

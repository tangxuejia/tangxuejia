How the bar count is calculated

The calculator subtracts both edge clearances, divides the usable width by the selected spacing, rounds up, and adds one bar for the starting edge.

usable width = slab width − 2 × edge clearance
bars = ceil(usable width ÷ spacing) + 1

Planning only: final reinforcement layout, bar size, cover, lap lengths and maximum spacing limits must come from the structural drawings and applicable local requirements.

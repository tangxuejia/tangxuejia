How to calculate baseboard

Start with the room perimeter, subtract door openings where baseboard will not be installed, add waste for miters and mistakes, then round up to full pieces.

Pieces = ceiling(((2 × length + 2 × width − openings) × (1 + waste)) ÷ piece length)

Practical planning tips

Buy matching trim from the same profile and lot when possible. Extra length is useful for inside corners, outside corners, scarf joints and damaged cuts.

FAQ

Should closets be included?

Include only the walls where baseboard will actually be installed. Measure closets separately when the trim plan differs.

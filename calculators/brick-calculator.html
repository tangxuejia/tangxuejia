Why mortar joints matter

Brick quantity is based on the effective module: brick face dimension plus the mortar joint. This is more useful than dividing wall area by the raw brick face alone.

net wall area = length × height − openings
module area = (brick length + joint) × (brick height + joint)
bricks = net wall area ÷ module area × (1 + waste)

Important limitation

Brickwork can use different bonds, cuts, returns, corners and openings. Use the actual brick dimensions and intended joint from the project specification. This tool is a planning estimate, not a masonry takeoff.

FAQ

Can I use any brick size?

Yes. Enter the actual face dimensions of the brick you plan to purchase.

Should I include doors and windows?

Enter their combined area in the openings field so the calculator subtracts it from the wall.

How much waste should I allow?

Use a project-appropriate allowance for cuts, breakage and future replacements rather than assuming one percentage always fits.

Brick Calculator

Estimate the number of bricks required for your wall, patio, or masonry project.

How to calculate bricks needed

First calculate the wall area, subtract doors and windows, then divide the remaining area by the coverage of one brick.

Bricks needed = Wall area ÷ Brick coverage

Example

A larger wall requires more bricks. Always add extra bricks for cutting, breakage, and future repairs.

Tips for accurate estimates

Brick size, mortar joints, pattern, and local construction methods can change the final quantity.

FAQ

How many bricks do I need?

Measure your wall area and divide by the coverage per brick.

Should I buy extra bricks?

Yes. Many projects include additional bricks for waste and replacements.

Does brick size affect the calculation?

Yes. Different brick dimensions change the number required per square meter or square foot.

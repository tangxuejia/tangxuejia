How the square feet calculator works

For a rectangle, area is length multiplied by width. If you have several equal areas, multiply that result by the quantity. The calculator also converts the total into square yards and square meters.

area = length × width

Example

A 12 × 10 ft rectangle contains 120 ft², 13.33 yd², and about 11.15 m².

Irregular spaces: divide an L-shaped or irregular room into simple rectangles, calculate each section, then add the areas together.

Square feet calculator FAQ

How do I calculate square feet?

Multiply length in feet by width in feet.

How many square feet are in a square yard?

There are 9 square feet in 1 square yard.

Can this calculate several identical rooms?

Yes. Use the quantity input to multiply the same rectangle by the number of equal areas.

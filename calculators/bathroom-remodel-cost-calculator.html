What this estimate includes

The rate represents a broad bathroom remodel allowance. Fixtures and upgrades are separate so you can adjust for a vanity, shower system, tub, toilet, lighting, or plumbing changes instead of hiding them in one number.

total = room area × finish rate + upgrades, then × (1 + contingency)

Check before quoting: waterproofing, hidden water damage, electrical changes, permits, accessibility work, and moving plumbing can change the final price substantially.

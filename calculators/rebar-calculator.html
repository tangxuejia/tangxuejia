Rebar Calculator

Estimate reinforcement material from project dimensions and planned bar spacing.

How it works

Enter the area dimensions, choose a planning spacing, and allow for cutting and overlap waste. This produces an approximate material quantity, not a structural design.

Formula

Number of bars ≈ span ÷ spacing + 1. Total rebar length is then rounded for cuts, overlaps, and purchase lengths.

FAQ

Does this replace structural plans?

No. Rebar size, spacing, laps, cover, and structural requirements must come from project plans, local requirements, or a qualified professional.

Should I include waste?

Yes. Cutting and overlap requirements can increase the amount ordered, so keep the allowance editable.

How the rebar estimate works

The calculator places one bar grid in each direction. It counts bars across each usable span, adds the editable waste/lap allowance to total linear length, then rounds the result to your stock bar length.

bars across = ceil((usable span ÷ spacing)) + 1
total length = direction A length + direction B length, then × (1 + allowance)

Use the scope selector honestly

A cosmetic refresh may leave layout and utilities in place, while a full renovation can include demolition, new systems, permits, and finish work. The scope choice usually matters more than a small change in the finish factor.

budget = area × scope rate × finish factor + other costs, then add contingency

Planning only: this is not a quote. Ask contractors to separate labor, materials, permits, demolition, disposal, and allowances so you can compare bids.

Renovation Cost Calculator

Estimate a home remodel budget based on area, renovation level, and cost assumptions.

How the renovation cost calculator works

The estimate uses home size, renovation scope, and average cost assumptions to create a planning budget.

estimated cost = renovation area × cost per square foot

Example

A 1,000 square foot renovation at a selected cost range gives a starting budget estimate before design changes and local labor differences.

Factors that change renovation cost

  • Location and labor rates
  • Material quality
  • Project complexity
  • Structural changes

FAQ

Is this a final contractor quote?

No. It is a planning estimate. Confirm prices with local professionals and suppliers.

What affects remodel cost most?

Labor, materials, project scope, and unexpected site conditions can significantly change costs.

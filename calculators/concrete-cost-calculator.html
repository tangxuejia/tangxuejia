Concrete Cost Calculator

Estimate the material cost of concrete projects including slabs, driveways, patios, and footings.

How to Calculate Concrete Cost

First calculate the required concrete volume. Then multiply the volume by the local concrete price. Add delivery, waste, and project-specific costs for a more realistic estimate.

Formula

Total cost = concrete volume × price per unit + delivery + additional allowance.

Example

A project requiring several cubic yards of concrete can be estimated by multiplying the required volume by the supplier price per cubic yard, then adding delivery and extra material allowance.

Common Uses

  • Driveways
  • Patios
  • Concrete slabs
  • Foundation footings

FAQ

Does this include labor cost?

This calculator estimates material-related costs. Labor depends on location, contractor rates, and project complexity.

Why should I add extra concrete?

Waste, uneven ground, cutting, and measurement differences can increase the final amount needed.

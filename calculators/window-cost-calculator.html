How the estimate works

The planning total combines the window unit allowance with installation per opening, then applies a contingency for trim, disposal, flashing, access, and unexpected conditions.

Total = windows × (unit price + installation) × (1 + contingency)

Important notes

Window type, size, glazing, frame material, energy rating, structural repairs and local labor rates can change the final price substantially. Use this result for early budgeting only.

FAQ

Does this include permits?

No. Permit, engineering and disposal costs should be confirmed locally.

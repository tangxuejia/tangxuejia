How slope is calculated

Slope percentage uses vertical rise divided by horizontal run. The angle uses the arctangent of the same ratio.

grade (%) = rise ÷ run × 100
angle = arctan(rise ÷ run)

Worked example

A 6 inch rise over a 120 inch horizontal run is a 5% grade and an angle of about 2.86°.

How foundation volume is calculated

Measure each simple foundation section as length × width × depth, convert the volume, then add an editable allowance for waste and uneven excavation.

volume = length × width × depth
order volume = net volume × (1 + allowance)

Planning only: soil bearing, footing size, reinforcement, frost depth, drainage, and structural requirements must come from the project drawings and local requirements.

Concrete Driveway Calculator

Estimate the concrete needed for a driveway slab before requesting supplier quotes.

How to calculate driveway concrete

Measure the driveway length, width, and planned slab thickness. Convert the resulting volume to cubic yards, then add a practical waste allowance for uneven ground and ordering differences.

Formula

Cubic yards = length × width × thickness in feet ÷ 27. Final order quantity = raw volume × (1 + waste allowance).

FAQ

Does this include reinforcement?

No. Reinforcement, sub-base, drainage, joints, and thickness requirements should follow the project design and local requirements.

How much extra should I order?

Use an editable allowance and confirm the final quantity with the concrete supplier.

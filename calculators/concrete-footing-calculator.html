Concrete Footing Calculator

Estimate the amount of concrete needed for foundation footings, deck posts, fences and other support structures.

How to Calculate Concrete for Footings

Calculate footing volume using length × width × depth for rectangular footings. Round footings use the cylinder volume formula.

Formula

Rectangular footing volume = length × width × depth.

Concrete required = calculated volume + waste allowance.

Example

A footing 2 ft long, 2 ft wide and 1 ft deep requires 4 cubic feet of concrete before adding extra material.

Common Uses

  • Deck foundations
  • Fence posts
  • Small building foundations
  • Concrete supports

Tips

  • Add extra material to account for waste.
  • Check local building requirements before construction.

FAQ

How much concrete do I need for a footing?

The amount depends on footing size, depth and the number of footings required.

Should I add extra concrete?

Yes, a small waste allowance helps avoid shortages during construction.

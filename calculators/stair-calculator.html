Stair Calculator

Estimate the number of risers and average riser height from total stair rise.

Enter dimensions and calculate.

The riser count is rounded to a whole number, then the actual average riser is total rise divided by that count. Tread count is commonly one fewer than risers for a stair flight, but landings and stair configurations vary.

Important: stair geometry is code-sensitive. Check your local building code, landing requirements, handrails, guards and maximum/minimum dimensions before construction.

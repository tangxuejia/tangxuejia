How rebar weight is calculated

US bar sizes use published nominal weights in pounds per foot. Metric bars use the common theoretical relationship based on diameter squared.

total weight = bar count × length × unit weight
metric kg/m ≈ diameter² ÷ 162

Planning only: this tool estimates material weight. Bar size, spacing, laps, hooks, cover and reinforcement design must come from the project drawings and applicable local requirements.

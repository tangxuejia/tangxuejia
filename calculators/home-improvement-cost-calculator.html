How the budget range works

This tool separates area-based work from additional costs and contingency. Rates are planning assumptions, not contractor bids, and should be replaced with local supplier and labor prices.

total range = (area × project rate × finish factor + extras) × (1 + contingency)

Important: permits, design fees, structural repairs, hazardous-material abatement, utility upgrades, and regional labor differences can materially change the final budget.

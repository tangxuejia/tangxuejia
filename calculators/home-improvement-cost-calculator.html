Home Improvement Cost Calculator

Estimate your remodeling budget before starting a home improvement project.

How it works

The estimate combines project area, renovation type, material quality and labor costs.

Estimated Cost = Project Area × Cost Per Square Foot + Additional Costs

Example

A 1,000 sq ft renovation with medium-quality materials can be estimated by multiplying the area by an average renovation cost range and adding special upgrades.

Cost Factors

  • Project scope
  • Materials selected
  • Labor rates
  • Location
  • Design changes

FAQ

Does this replace a contractor quote?

No. It is a planning estimate to help prepare a realistic budget.

Why do costs vary?

Labor, materials and project complexity can significantly change final costs.

Should I include extra budget?

Many homeowners reserve additional funds for unexpected changes.

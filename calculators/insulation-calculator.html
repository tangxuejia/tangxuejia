How insulation quantity is calculated

Measure the surface area, add a cutting and fitting allowance, then divide by the coverage printed on the product package. The calculator rounds up to complete packages.

Packages = ceiling((area × (1 + waste)) ÷ package coverage)

Choose the right product

R-value, thickness, vapor-control requirements, moisture exposure, framing depth and local energy codes matter. Never select insulation by price alone; check the manufacturer label and local requirements.

FAQ

What waste allowance should I use?

Ten percent works as a starting point for simple rectangular areas. Use more for irregular layouts or many obstructions.

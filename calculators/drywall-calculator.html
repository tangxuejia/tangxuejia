How the estimate works

Net surface area equals wall area minus measured openings. One 4 × 8 ft sheet covers 32 square feet. The calculator applies your waste allowance, rounds sheets up, and provides separate planning estimates for screws and joint compound.

Sheets = ceiling((length × height − openings) × (1 + waste) ÷ 32)

Planning notes

  • Use actual board dimensions when your supplier sells a different size.
  • Ten percent waste suits many simple rooms; complex cuts may need more.
  • Screw and compound estimates vary with framing, board thickness, finish level and local practice.
  • Confirm moisture, fire-rating and code requirements before buying.

FAQ

How many square feet does a 4 × 8 sheet cover?

It covers 32 square feet before openings and cutting waste.

Does this include ceilings?

Yes. Enter the ceiling's length and width as the two surface dimensions.

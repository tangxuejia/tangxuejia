Estimate drywall sheets, screws and joint compound for a wall or ceiling. The default sheet is 4 × 8 ft, a common US size.

Enter dimensions and calculate.

How it works

Net area = wall area − openings. Sheets = net area × (1 + waste) ÷ 32 sq ft. Screw and compound allowances are estimates; actual quantities depend on framing, board thickness, finish level and local practice.

FAQ

Should I include waste? Yes. 10% is a reasonable starting point for many simple rooms, while complex layouts can need more.

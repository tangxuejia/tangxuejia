How the estimate works

Thickness is converted from inches to feet, then length × width × thickness gives the base volume. The calculator adds the selected overage after calculating the base quantity.

base ft³ = length × width × (thickness ÷ 12)
order yd³ = base ft³ × (1 + waste ÷ 100) ÷ 27

Worked example

A 20 ft × 10 ft slab at 4 in thick is about 66.7 ft³, or 2.47 yd³ before waste. With 7% overage, the planning quantity is about 2.64 yd³.

Before ordering

Ready-mix suppliers normally quote by cubic yard. Confirm yield, minimum load, delivery rules, reinforcement, subgrade and final field measurements.

FAQ

How thick should a slab be?

Thickness depends on loads, soil, reinforcement, climate and applicable requirements. This tool calculates volume; it does not design the slab.

Why add waste?

Uneven subgrade, forms, spillage and measurement differences can create a shortage. Use an allowance that fits the project.

Can I use metric dimensions?

This page defaults to US customary units because concrete ordering is commonly quoted in cubic yards in the US.

How to calculate concrete for a slab

Measure the slab length, width and thickness. The calculator uses these measurements to estimate concrete volume and material requirements.

Formula

Volume = length × width × thickness

Worked example

A 12 ft × 10 ft slab with 4 inch thickness requires approximately 1.48 cubic yards of concrete before adding waste allowance.

Common slab projects

  • Patio slabs
  • Driveways
  • Garage floors
  • Concrete pads

Tips before ordering concrete

  • Measure thickness carefully.
  • Add a reasonable waste allowance.
  • Confirm supplier coverage and delivery requirements.

FAQ

How thick should a concrete slab be?

Thickness depends on the project use, local requirements and design conditions.

Should I order extra concrete?

Most projects include some extra material for waste, uneven ground and measurement differences.

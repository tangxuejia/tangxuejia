About RenoMetric

Transparent calculators for practical home projects.

Why RenoMetric exists

RenoMetric helps homeowners and DIYers estimate material quantities before buying. Each calculator combines working math with a plain-English explanation of the formula, editable assumptions, and purchase rounding.

How the site is funded

The site is designed to be supported in the future by display advertising and clearly disclosed affiliate links. Monetization does not change calculator formulas.

Independence

RenoMetric is not a contractor, building authority, product manufacturer, retailer, or engineering service. Estimates are planning aids and should be checked against actual project conditions and product documentation.

1. Geometry first

We start with measurable geometry: area, volume, slope, perimeter, or spacing. Unit conversions are applied before material-specific assumptions.

2. Assumptions stay editable

Coverage rates, waste, density, package coverage, board width, spacing, and similar factors can vary by project. When they materially affect a result, the user can change them.

3. Purchase units are rounded separately

A geometric requirement and a shopping quantity are different numbers. Boxes, bags, bundles, and whole posts are rounded only after the raw requirement is calculated.

4. Human review gate

Programmatic publishing is useful for scale, but a page should not be indexed just because a template can generate it. Each page must add a real calculation, example, explanation, or decision aid.

5. Safety boundary

RenoMetric does not choose structural dimensions, code limits, engineering factors, or legally required specifications.

Calculator inputs

Calculator values are processed in your browser. The MVP does not send project measurements to a RenoMetric database.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, information you submit may be processed by the hosting provider so the site operator can respond.

Analytics and advertising

Google AdSense ad code is not active in the MVP. If analytics or advertising is enabled later, this policy will be updated before activation to describe providers, cookies or similar technologies, consent choices, and opt-out controls.

Third parties

Hosting and security providers may process standard technical request data such as IP address, browser information, and request logs as part of delivering and protecting the website.

Planning estimates only

Results depend on the measurements and assumptions entered. Actual material needs can differ due to installation method, waste, product yield, site conditions, packaging, workmanship, and measurement error.

No structural or code advice

Do not use RenoMetric to select structural dimensions, code-required spacing, safety factors, load capacity, or legally required specifications. Obtain those values from the applicable design, manufacturer, authority, supplier, or qualified professional.

Verify before purchasing

Check actual product coverage, yield, package quantity, and project conditions before placing an order.
